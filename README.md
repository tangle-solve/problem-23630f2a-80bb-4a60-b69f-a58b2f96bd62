### CHEM-000036: Universal tacticity control in radical polymerization
- **Subfield:** Polymer chemistry and plastics circularity
- **Open:** Control tacticity across diverse vinyl monomers, including strongly polar substituents, without requiring special bulky pendant groups followed by their replacement.
- **Status:** A longstanding gap. Pan and Ouchi obtained approximately 99% meso-dyad polyacrylamides in 2025 using a transformable bulky pendant; general direct control remains open.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Combines practical radical processing with precision thermal and mechanical properties.
- **Key works:** Pan and Ouchi (2025), *Post-polymerization modification enabling library synthesis of highly isotactic polyacrylamides carrying different pendant groups*, [DOI:10.1038/s42004-025-01663-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-025-01663-3).
